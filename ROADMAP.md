# AGI-1 Roadmap

## Phase 1: Prototype Core

- Perception, memory, reasoning, planning, execution
- Validation: one complete cognitive cycle persists state and emits a dashboard

## Phase 2: World Model And Planning

- Causal simulation
- feasibility checks
- long-horizon scenario planning
- Validation: scenario forecasts and plan simulation agree on bounded actions

## Phase 3: Learning And Feedback

- reward scoring
- error analysis
- online adaptation
- Validation: successful actions improve future plan selection

## Phase 4: Self Model And Belief System

- identity persistence
- belief updates
- contradiction detection
- Validation: self-model reflects new evidence without internal inconsistency

## Phase 5: Strategic Intelligence And Research

- long-horizon optimizer
- goal hierarchy
- autonomous hypothesis and experiment planning
- Validation: research loop generates and evaluates novel questions

## Phase 6: Value Alignment And Moral Reasoning

- value model
- consequence analysis
- alignment checks
- Validation: unsafe or misaligned plans are blocked before execution

## Phase 7: Curiosity, Creativity, And Imagination

- novelty-seeking
- concept synthesis
- counterfactual simulation
- Validation: the system proposes informative explorations and novel strategies

## Phase 8: Mathematical Optimization And Meta-Intelligence

- Bayesian belief updates
- objective optimization
- strategy selection
- Validation: reasoning confidence, alignment, and stability improve over time

## Phase 9: Recursive Expansion

- supervisor-scale orchestration
- research lab coordination
- architecture self-improvement proposals
- Validation: measurable swarm and lab metrics persist as runtime artifacts
